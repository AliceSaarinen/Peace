### Karjalanpiirakat | 7 | 4 | ruisjauho,riisi | |
Karjalanpiirakat
Puuro:
2 dl puuroriisiä
4 dl vettä
1 l maitoa
1 tl suolaa
Kuori:
2 dl vettä
1 tl suolaa
5 dl ruisjauhoja
1. Keitä riisi vedessä, lisää maito ja hauduta paksuksi puuroksi. Mausta suolalla.

### Banana bread | 8 | 3 | banana,flour | |
Banana Bread
3 ripe bananas
1/3 cup melted butter
3/4 cup sugar
1 egg, beaten
1 tsp vanilla extract
1 tsp baking soda
pinch of salt
1 1/2 cups flour
Preheat the oven to 350°F and grease a loaf pan.
Mash the bananas and mix in the butter, sugar, egg and vanilla.
Stir in the soda, salt and flour, pour into the pan and bake for 60 minutes.
### Chicken curry | 8 | 3 | onion,garlic,coconut | |
Chicken Curry
500 g chicken thighs
1 onion, diced
3 cloves garlic
1 tbsp ginger, grated
2 tbsp curry powder
400 ml coconut milk
1 cup chicken stock
2 tbsp ghee
Fry the onion, garlic and ginger in the ghee until soft.

Pour in the coconut milk and stock and simmer for 20 minutes. Serve with rice.

### Lasagne | 9 | 4 | lasagne,tomato | |
Lasagne
500 g minced beef
1 onion
400 g canned tomatoes
2 tbsp tomato paste
50 g butter
50 g flour
600 ml milk
12 lasagne sheets
100 g mozzarella
Brown the beef with the onion, add the tomatoes and paste and simmer for 20 minutes.
Melt the butter, stir in the flour, then whisk in the milk to make a white sauce.
Layer the meat sauce, lasagne sheets and white sauce in a dish and top with mozzarella.
Bake at 190°C for 40 minutes.
### Chocolate chip cookies | 9 | 3 | flour,chocolate | |
Chocolate Chip Cookies
1 cup butter, softened
1 cup white sugar
1 cup brown sugar
2 eggs
2 tsp vanilla extract
1 tsp baking soda
1/2 tsp salt
3 cups all-purpose flour
2 cups chocolate chips
Cream the butter and sugars, then beat in the eggs and vanilla.
Mix in the soda, salt and flour, then stir in the chocolate chips.
Drop spoonfuls onto a baking sheet and bake at 175°C for 10 minutes.
