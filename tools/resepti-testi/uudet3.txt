### Korvapuustit | 8 | 4 | hiiva,kaneli | |
Korvapuustit
Taikina:
2,5 dl maitoa
25 g hiivaa
1 dl sokeria
1 tl kardemummaa
7 dl vehnäjauhoja
100 g voita
Täyte:
75 g voita
2 rkl kanelia
1. Lämmitä maito ja liuota hiiva. Lisää sokeri, kardemumma ja jauhot.
2. Vaivaa voi taikinaan ja anna kohota.
3. Kauli levyksi, levitä pehmeä voi ja kaneli, kääri rullaksi ja leikkaa korvapuusteiksi.
4. Paista 225 asteessa 10 minuuttia.

### Sticky toffee pudding | 9 | 3 | dates,sugar | |
Sticky Toffee Pudding
For the sponge:
200 g dates, chopped
1 tsp baking soda
175 g self-raising flour
85 g butter
140 g brown sugar
2 eggs
For the sauce:
175 g brown sugar
50 g butter
225 ml double cream
Soak the dates in 300 ml boiling water with the soda.
Cream the butter and sugar, beat in the eggs, fold in the flour and dates and bake at 180°C for 30 minutes.
Heat the sugar, butter and cream until bubbling and pour over the pudding.

### Blueberry muffins (blog) | 8 | 3 | blueberries,flour | |
The Best Blueberry Muffins
These muffins are my go-to recipe for lazy Sunday mornings. They are fluffy, moist and packed with berries!
Ingredients
2 cups all-purpose flour
2 tsp baking powder
1/2 tsp salt
3/4 cup sugar
1/2 cup butter, melted
2 eggs
1/2 cup milk
1 1/2 cups blueberries
Instructions
Preheat the oven to 375°F and line a muffin tin.
Whisk the dry ingredients, then stir in the butter, eggs and milk.
Fold in the blueberries, fill the cups and bake for 22 minutes.
### Pad thai | 8 | 3 | noodles,peanuts | |
Pad Thai
200 g rice noodles
200 g shrimp
2 eggs
2 tbsp fish sauce
2 tbsp tamarind paste
1 tbsp sugar
100 g bean sprouts
50 g peanuts
Soak the noodles in warm water for 20 minutes.
Stir-fry the shrimp, push aside and scramble the eggs.
Add the noodles, fish sauce, tamarind and sugar, toss with the sprouts and top with peanuts.
